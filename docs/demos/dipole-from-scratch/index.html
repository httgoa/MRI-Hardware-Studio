Freeze the physics, geometry, and evidence contract before execution

Define a reproducible matched-reference experiment

The task is a workflow-validation case, not a coil-design optimization study. A regular dipole, support stack, and homogeneous tissue load are modeled at a 210 MHz target frequency; the declared output is accepted-power-normalized B1+.

STAGE 03 · RF MATCHING & B1+ PIPELINE

Connect circuit performance to a power-normalized transmit field

Tune the feed network, run AC co-simulation, and reconstruct B1+

The 3D antenna model is coupled to a series/shunt inductive L-match. The tuned circuit then drives the AC field solution used for HDF5 export and circularly polarized B1+ reconstruction.

The series inductance was refined from 86 to 88 nH while the 37 nH shunt inductance remained fixed. The resulting match exceeds the predefined −20 dB criterion.

NORMALIZATION IS NOT OPTIONAL

The AC field was generated with approximately 50 W accepted power. Because B1+ scales with √P, reporting the raw field would overstate the 1 W transmit field by about √50. Every quantitative result below uses the measured accepted power.

ROI CONTROL

Exclude the conductor singularity from the performance metric

The full export contains a 64.96 µT maximum adjacent to the PEC conductor. That value is a near-field sampling artifact for this comparison. The reported 1.485 µT/√W peak is restricted to Z ≤ −24 mm inside the phantom.
