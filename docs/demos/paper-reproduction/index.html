WHY THESE RULES?

“A solver completion is not evidence by itself.”

Every case also requires traceable result-tree sources, finite positive accepted power, finite normalized B1+/SAR quantities, complete longitudinal ROI coverage, and an export audit. A completed solver run without retrievable field evidence is not treated as validated.
